AAIYYANMATH MATHEMATICAL GRAMMAR (EBNF, implemented by crates/parser)

program      = { separator } , { statement , { separator } } ;
separator    = NEWLINE | ";" ;
statement    = lvalue , ":=" , expr | expr ;
lvalue       = IDENT | IDENT , "(" , [ IDENT , { "," , IDENT } ] , ")" ;

expr         = conditional | binary ;
conditional  = "if" , expr , "then" , expr , "else" , expr
             | "if" , "(" , expr , "," , expr , "," , expr , ")" ;
binary       = prefix , { operator , prefix | implicit_mul | range } ;
range        = ".." , expr ;
implicit_mul = prefix            (* juxtaposition, e.g. 5 m, 2 x, 10 kN/m *) ;

prefix       = NUMBER | STRING | IDENT
             | "-" , prefix | "+" , prefix | "not" , prefix
             | "(" , expr , ")" | bracket ;
bracket      = "[" , [ expr , { "," , expr } ] , "]"
             | "[" , row , { "," , row } , "]" ;
row          = "[" , expr , { "," , expr } , "]" ;
postfix      = "(" , [ args ] , ")" | "[" , indices , "]" | "'" | "!" ;

operator     = "or" | "and" | "=" | "==" | "!=" | "<" | "<=" | ">" | ">="
             | "+" | "-" | "*" | "/" | "mod" | "%" | ".*" | "./" | "^" | ".^" ;

Precedence (low to high):
  1 conditional, 2 or, 4 and, 6 comparisons, 8 range, 10 additive,
  12 multiplicative, 14 implicit multiplication, 16 unary, 18 power (right assoc),
  20 postfix (call, index, transpose, factorial)

Lexical rules:
  numbers     123, 1.5, .5, 8.5e-6
  identifiers unicode letters, digits and '_' (Greek letters supported)
  comments    '#' or '//' to end of line
  strings     double quotes with backslash escapes
  newlines    statement separators, ignored inside brackets

Semantics notes:
  ':=' is assignment; '=' is equality inside expressions.
  Indexing is 0-based (matches NumPy).
  '*' is matrix multiplication for matrices, dot product for two vectors.
  '.*', './', '.^' are element-wise operations.
  Unit names resolve through the unit registry unless shadowed by a variable.
